# Session archive

Historical one-line session headlines compressed from `PROGRESS.md`. The full retrospective for any session
survives in `git log -p PROGRESS.md` at that session's compression commit.

`PROGRESS.md` archives its oldest entry here once its detail band holds more than 5 sessions or exceeds roughly
10k tokens (see `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` §Session handoff).
